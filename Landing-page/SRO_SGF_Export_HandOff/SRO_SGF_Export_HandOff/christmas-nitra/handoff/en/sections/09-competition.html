Competition programmes

Individuals and groups

Rhythmic gymnastics with apparatus — rope, hoop, ball, clubs and ribbon.

IndividualsGroupsRopeHoopBallClubsRibbon
WG: the all-around with hoop, ball, clubs and ribbon doubles as qualification; on Saturday the top 8 per apparatus go on to the apparatus finals. Open: individuals perform up to 2 routines by year of birth, groups by category.
