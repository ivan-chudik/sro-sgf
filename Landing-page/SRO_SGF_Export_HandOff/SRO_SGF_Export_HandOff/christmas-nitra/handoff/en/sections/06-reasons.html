Why Christmas Nitra

Three reasons to book this weekend

01

World-class field

An official World Gymnastics event and an international invitational competition — gymnasts from across Europe and the world, judges with international brevets. Routines you usually only see on TV, watched from the stands.

02

27 years of tradition

One of the longest-running rhythmic gymnastics competitions in Slovakia — the 27th edition under the banner of ŠK ŠOG Nitra and the Slovak Gymnastics Federation.

03

Nitra before Christmas

Gymnastics, music and a festive atmosphere — the perfect family weekend before Christmas. Four days of programme, pick the one that's yours.
