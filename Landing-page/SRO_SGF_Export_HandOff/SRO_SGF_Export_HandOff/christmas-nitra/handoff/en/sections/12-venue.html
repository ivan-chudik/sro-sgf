Venue

Nitra, 26 – 29 November

Nitra City Sports HallDolnočermánska 105, 949 01 Nitra – Klokočina, Slovakia
Getting there

Under 60 minutes by car from Bratislava, about 90 minutes from Vienna. The hall is in the Klokočina district, a few minutes from Nitra's centre.

Parking

Parking right at the hall on Dolnočermánska street.

Entry

Doors open one hour before the first session. Just show your e-ticket with QR code on your phone.

For visitors

Your ticket is valid all day — leave between sessions and come back. Festive Nitra is a few minutes from the hall.

The hall

One competition floor and two training floors, 15 m ceiling height — the home of Christmas Nitra.

Accommodation

Partner hotels with the code “Christmas Nitra 2026”: H11, Centrum, City, OKO and Zobor.
